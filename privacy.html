In short

  • We collect what we need to run LAKAI: your account, payments, your uploads and creations, and usage data.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising, and we do not train AI models on your private files.
  • Uploads and prompts are sent to the AI providers needed to complete the task you asked for.
  • Analytics cookies only run if you allow them. Change your choice at any time in Your privacy choices.
  • To access, export or delete your data, email support@lakai.in.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how LAKAI ("LAKAI", "we", "us") handles personal data when you use lakai.pro, our apps, the LAKAI MCP connector and related services (the "Service"). LAKAI is the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us about privacy at support@lakai.in.

2. What we collect

Data you give us

  • Account data. Name, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider) and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and photo from Google.
  • Your content. Prompts, scripts, chat messages, and images, video, audio and other files you upload or save to your Assets library, plus the outputs we generate for you, your projects and your generation history.
  • Voice and likeness data. Voice samples you upload to clone a voice, and photos or videos of people that you use in generation. See our Biometric Data Notice.
  • Billing data. Plan, purchase history, credit balance, trial and auto-refill settings, and billing country. Our payment processors collect your card or other payment details directly. We never receive full card numbers.
  • Communications. Messages you send to support, the support chat or Discord, survey answers, and feedback.
  • Connected accounts. If you connect a YouTube channel, we receive the channel information and analytics you authorise. We never receive your Google password.

Data we collect automatically

  • Usage data. Pages and tools used, generations started, credits spent, errors, and timing.
  • Device and log data. IP address, approximate location from your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, and timestamps.
  • Security signals. A device identifier and browser characteristics. We use these only to prevent fraud, trial abuse and duplicate accounts.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy. Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if you allow them.
  • Referral data. If you arrive through a partner campaign link, we record which link it was so we can measure that campaign.

4. Automated decisions

Automated systems check generation requests against our content rules and flag possible fraud. A request that fails a check may be blocked automatically. Account-level actions, such as suspension, are reviewed by a person. To ask for human review of a decision, email support@lakai.in.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers (subprocessors). These include hosting, storage, database, authentication, email, analytics and error monitoring providers. They act on our instructions. The full list is on our Subprocessors page.
  • AI model providers. When you run a tool, we send the prompt and files needed for that task to the provider that runs the chosen model. For example, we send a voice sample to the voice provider, or an image and prompt to the video model you picked. These providers process the data to return your result, under their own terms and data policies.
  • Payment processors. They process your payments and may act as merchant of record.
  • People you choose. If you publish to Community or share a link, anyone with access can see that content.
  • Legal and safety. We share data with authorities when the law requires it or to protect people. See our Law Enforcement Guidelines.
  • Business transfers. If LAKAI is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, data may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

6. Google and YouTube data

If you connect a YouTube account, we use the YouTube API Services, and the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. We use Google user data only to show you your own channel analytics and insights inside LAKAI. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train general AI models. Our use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke our access at any time in your Google security settings.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted storage at our cloud providers, access controls, short-lived signed links to your files and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. International transfers

We and our providers process data in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from where you live. When we transfer personal data out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on adequacy decisions, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum), or another lawful transfer mechanism.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data and your account;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already happened;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data, or of targeted advertising (we do neither);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information;
  • not be treated differently for exercising your rights; and
  • complain to your data protection authority.

We give these rights to all users, wherever they live. To use them, email support@lakai.in from your account email, or use Your privacy choices. We may need to verify your identity first. We reply within 30 days, or within 45 days where US state law allows, and we will tell you if we need longer. You can use an authorised agent with your signed permission. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we will answer the appeal within the legal timeframe.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out, and we keep analytics and marketing cookies off for that browser.

California and other US states

In the last 12 months, we have collected the categories of personal information described in section 2: identifiers, commercial information, internet activity, approximate geolocation, audio and visual information, and inferences about tool use. Voice samples can be sensitive personal information. We use them only to provide the voice feature you asked for. We collect this information for the purposes in section 3 and disclose it to the categories of recipients in section 5. We have not sold or shared personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not knowingly sell or share the data of anyone under 16.

11. Children

LAKAI is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email support@lakai.in and we will delete it.

12. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies and storage to run the Service. We use analytics and marketing cookies only if you allow them. The full list and how to change your choice are in our Cookie Policy.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

14. Contact

For questions or requests about privacy, contact LAKAI at support@lakai.in.
