LAKAI vs Clippie.ai: Which Auto-Clip Tool Wins in 2026?

Both turn long videos into shorts, but they take very different approaches. Here's the head-to-head.

Clippie.ai and LAKAI both promise to turn long-form content into viral shorts. But Clippie is narrowly an auto-clipping tool, while LAKAI bundles auto-clipping with template-based content generation.

What each does


Clippie: Upload a podcast/long video. Clippie picks viral moments and exports 9:16 shorts with captions.
LAKAI Auto Clip: Same workflow, plus LAKAI's other templates (Faceless, Character Explainer, etc.) and 15+ creator tools, all on one credit pool.

Quality of viral moment detection


Both use AI to find peak moments. LAKAI runs OpenAI scene-finding plus AssemblyAI transcription, weighting hooks, controversy, and audience reaction. Clippie uses a similar combination. In our tests, both pull comparable clip quality from the same input.

Captions


LAKAI offers word-by-word burned-in captions with multiple style presets. Clippie offers similar styles but fewer customization knobs.

Pricing


LAKAI Starter ($19): 25 auto clips + 20 faceless + 20 character explainer + etc.
Clippie: starts around $20 for clipping alone, no other formats.

If you want clipping plus the ability to make Faceless or Character Explainer shorts in the same subscription, LAKAI is the obvious choice.

Verdict


Clippie wins if you only ever clip long content and want a single-purpose tool.
LAKAI wins if you also want to make original shorts, run faceless channels, or use AI tools beyond clipping.

For 90% of creators, the broader LAKAI stack at a similar price is the better deal.
