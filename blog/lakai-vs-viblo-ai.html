LAKAI vs Viblo AI: The Faceless Video Showdown

Viblo AI markets to faceless creators with cinematic visuals. Here's how it actually compares to LAKAI.

Viblo AI's pitch is cinematic AI footage stitched with narration. LAKAI's pitch is templated short-form content optimized for retention.

Visual style


Viblo: AI-generated cinematic b-roll. Looks great in 5-second clips, gets repetitive across a 30-second short.
LAKAI: gameplay underlay (Minecraft, Subway Surfers, GTA) plus animated character explainers. The visual variety holds attention longer.

Templates


Viblo: narration with AI b-roll.
LAKAI: 5 distinct template styles.

Voice


Both use ElevenLabs. LAKAI unlimited on paid. Viblo has caps.

Caption sync


LAKAI uses Whisper word-by-word burned captions. Viblo uses sentence-level captions, which are less engaging for TikTok-style scrolling.

Pricing


Viblo: ~$25-40 entry.
LAKAI Starter: $19.

Verdict


For aesthetic-driven content, Viblo's AI footage looks impressive. For retention-driven content (the kind that actually monetizes), LAKAI's gameplay-underlay + word-by-word captions outperform in every short-form metric we've tested.
