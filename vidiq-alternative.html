What is vidIQ?

vidIQ is a browser extension that integrates into YouTube Studio, pulling live data from your channel to surface keyword and tag suggestions, bulk video management, and A/B thumbnail testing. It's built around your existing YouTube account, not around generating new content.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lakai cheaper than vidIQ?

We won't guess at vidIQ's current price here — check vidiq.com/pricing directly. Lakai starts at $19/mo (Starter) with a free plan to preview every tool first, so it's easy to compare against vidIQ's live pricing before deciding.

Does Lakai work inside YouTube Studio like vidIQ?

No. vidIQ integrates directly into YouTube Studio using your channel's live data. Lakai is a separate platform for generating scripts, voiceovers, and full videos with AI — it doesn't connect to your YouTube account or pull your channel analytics.

Can Lakai replace vidIQ entirely?

Not for keyword research pulled from your live channel data or bulk tag editing — vidIQ still wins there. But for creating new videos (scripts, voiceovers, thumbnails, Shorts), Lakai covers ground vidIQ doesn't touch at all.

Does Lakai have a free plan like vidIQ?

Lakai has a free account that lets you preview every tool before upgrading. Whether vidIQ's free tier compares favorably depends on its current terms — check vidiq.com/pricing, since free-tier limits change.

What does Lakai do that vidIQ doesn't?

Lakai generates content — AI scripts, cloned voiceovers, full videos across 10+ models (Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling), and one-click templates like faceless and fake-texts videos. vidIQ optimizes videos you've already made; it doesn't generate new ones.

Is Lakai good for thumbnail testing?

Lakai's Thumbnail Analyzer scores your thumbnail's CTR potential and suggests fixes before you upload. That's different from vidIQ's post-publish A/B testing between two live thumbnails — pick based on whether you want to test before or after you post.
