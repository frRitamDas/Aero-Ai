What is Viewmax?

Viewmax is an AI video creation platform built for high-volume, faceless, and UGC-style content creators. According to its own site (viewmax.io, checked directly before writing this page), it bundles an AI video generator — powered by models including Kling 3.0 Turbo and Seedance 2.0 — alongside an AI scriptwriter, AI voiceover, auto-captions with trending caption styles, a voice changer, a video editor, a video downloader, and a caption/watermark remover, all aimed at producing short-form videos for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram, and X quickly and without needing to appear on camera.

Viewmax also leans heavily into advertising and commerce use cases in a way Lakai doesn't. Its feature list includes a dedicated AI Ad Generator, an AI Ad Cloner (for replicating the structure of an existing ad with new creative), ad templates, a "Marketing Studio," and an AI Product Try-On tool that turns product photos into try-on style video ads. It also lists an AI Image Generator (referred to on-site as "Nano Banana Pro"), a "Gemini Omni Flash" video generation/editing feature, an "AI Clone" feature, and — like Lakai — its own MCP integration for connecting the platform to AI agents such as Claude.

The genuine overlap with Lakai is real, not manufactured for this page: both platforms offer AI video generation, AI scriptwriting, AI voiceover, auto-captions, and a caption/watermark remover, and both use some of the same underlying generation models (Kling and Seedance both appear in Lakai's own AI Video Generator model picker). Where the two diverge is scope. Viewmax's own site, as fetched, lists no pre-publish analysis tools — no thumbnail CTR scoring, no Shorts virality prediction, and no channel-wide "what to improve" analysis — while Viewmax's advertising and product-video tooling (Ad Cloner, Product Try-On, Marketing Studio) isn't something Lakai currently offers at all. Neither product is simply a worse version of the other; they're built around different jobs — Viewmax around volume content and ad creative, Lakai around a full creator pipeline that includes scoring your content before it goes out, not just generating it.

Where Lakai and Viewmax actually overlap: If turning product photos into video ads, cloning the structure of an existing ad, or running an ad-creative "Marketing Studio" is your actual use case, that's real functionality Viewmax has and Lakai does not — this page doesn't claim otherwise. The comparison below focuses on where Lakai has verifiably more to offer: pre-publish performance analysis (Thumbnail Analyzer, Shorts Audit, Viral Engine) that Viewmax's own site doesn't list as a feature, plus a broader 23-tool platform overall.
